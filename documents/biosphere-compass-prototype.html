The Process

How the Compass works

AI analysis of your public content, scored against the Biosphere Isle of Man framework and benchmarked against real businesses.

01

Submit your details

Enter your website URL, upload sustainability documents, and optionally answer a short questionnaire about your business.

02

AI analyses your content

Our AI reads your content against 30 indicators across 6 Biosphere dimensions, assessing the quality and depth of your sustainability evidence.

03

Receive your Compass report

Get a scored profile, benchmarked against the ideal Biosphere organisation and similar IoM businesses, with a clear pathway forward.
